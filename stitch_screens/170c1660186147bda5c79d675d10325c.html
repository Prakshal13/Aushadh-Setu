Frequently Asked Questions

Everything state missions ask us.

Does this replace existing e-Aushadhi or state inventory software? expand_more

No. Aushadh Setu acts as an intelligent overlay layer. It interfaces directly with CDAC's e-Aushadhi, state-specific portals (like Rajasthan's RMSCL or Maharashtra's e-Upkaran), and IDSP surveillance via read/write APIs, enhancing existing systems without requiring data re-entry.

How are medicines verified for cold-chain transit? expand_more

Every temperature-sensitive consignment (vaccines, anti-rabies, and sera) is logged with IoT data-logger verification. If a parcel breaches safe ranges during transit, the recipient PHC is automatically prompted to run quarantine verification before inventory absorption.

What is the typical deployment timeline for a pilot district? expand_more

Pilot deployments take approximately two to three weeks. Week 1 is dedicated to facility mapping and historical ledger ingestion; Week 2 covers DHO and pharmacist orientation; by Week 3, automated reallocation recommendations are active.

How is statutory drug auditing handled during inter-facility transfers? expand_more

All transfers produce a tamper-evident digital transfer voucher (DTV) adhering to General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017 and State Audit standards, eliminating manual register reconciliations.
