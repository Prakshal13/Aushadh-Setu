What is Autonomous Epidemic Redistribution?

Autonomous redistribution is an active digital health infrastructure layer that continuously reconciles clinical consumption trends against warehouse inventories. Rather than waiting for PHC pharmacists to manually compile paper stock indent sheets, the network detects impending epidemic curves and issues automated, GFR-compliant transfer manifests.

Proactive Surveillance Coupling

Prevents multimillion-rupee expired drug incinerations by rerouting near-dated batches from low-consumption taluks to high-velocity urban clinics.

Inquiries & Technical Protocol

Frequently asked questions

Common answers for State IT secretaries, Health Commissioners, and Chief Medical Officers.

Does Aushadh Setu replace our existing state e-Aushadhi system? expand_more

No. Aushadh Setu is engineered as an intelligent algorithmic layer that operates on top of existing e-Aushadhi or CDAC-designed state warehouse portals. It connects via standard ABDM-compliant FHIR APIs, continuously analyzing inventory without requiring your department to discard existing software or tender contracts.

How does the system ensure GFR 2017 financial audit compliance? expand_more

Every inter-district rebalance generates an authorized Electronic Inter-Store Transfer Voucher (e-ISTV) backed by Rule 149/154 provisions of GFR 2017. Both sending and receiving pharmacists sign using Aadhaar-based OTP verification, creating an unalterable log acceptable to State AG audits.

What is the standard deployment timeline for a high-risk district? expand_more

Our Rapid Pilot Deployment takes exactly 48 hours for data ingestion and facility mapping. Full operational integration—including frontline mobile orientation for up to 40 PHCs—is routinely accomplished within 7 working days without disrupting ongoing outpatient care.

How does cold-chain tracking operate in remote areas without internet? expand_more

Cold boxes utilize Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) temperature loggers that continuously record metrics internally for 90 days. When an ANM or van driver reaches any sub-center with intermittent 2G or 4G connectivity, data auto-syncs instantly into the state dashboard.

Can district collectors set manual overrides on automated transfers? expand_more

Yes. Aushadh Setu is decision-support infrastructure. District Collectors, CMOs, and State Health Secretaries maintain single-click veto and priority reallocation controls through an administrative crisis room dashboard at all times.

What data security certifications protect state clinical records? expand_more

All data resides strictly in sovereign Indian MeitY-empanelled cloud instances. The platform complies with ISO 27001, DISHA draft frameworks, and undergoes biannual security assessments by CERT-In accredited auditing agencies.
